Academia – Industry Partnership

Industry Internship Programme

The Industry Internship Programme places UG and PG students from Science and Engineering at industry and research partners for two-month summer internships, using the selection process of the Summer Research Fellowship Programme run jointly by IASc, INSA, and NASI. Applications open each October through the Academy websites.

Current Industry Partners

IISc–Axis Bank Centre for Mathematics & Computing

Starting from 2023, the Centre sponsors around 20 UG and PG students from Science and Engineering each year to undertake the Summer Research Fellowship at IISc laboratories across all disciplines.

Tata Consultancy Services

The TCS team selects candidates from a longlist sent by tAcT, who then undertake the programme at TCS Pune.

How to be considered

Candidates are shortlisted by the three-Academies' Science Education Panel Selection Committee comprising senior scientists, industry experts, and educators from across the country.

October

Applications open through the websites of IASc, INSA, and NASI as part of the Summer Research Fellowship Programme.

February–March

Shortlisted candidates are notified after the Science Education Panel selection process.

May–July

Selected students undertake two-month internships at Industry labs and scientific institutions across the country, with stipend, travel, and accommodation provided.
