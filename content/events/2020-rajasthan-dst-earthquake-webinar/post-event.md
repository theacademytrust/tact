## Earthquake and Challenges Webinar

A bilingual webinar on "Earthquake and challenges: Balance between nature and technology" was held on 17 June 2020, coordinated by the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Rajasthan, in association with The Academy Trust (tAcT).

**Speakers:**
- Dr. Vineet Gahalaut (Academy Fellow)
- Dr. CP Rajendran, JNCASR

**Moderator:** Ms. Mugdha Sinha, IAS, Secretary, DST Rajasthan

**Host:** Dr. Manu Sikarwar

The audio-visual bilingual talk elicited many questions from participants, with key issues discussed during the post-talk session.
