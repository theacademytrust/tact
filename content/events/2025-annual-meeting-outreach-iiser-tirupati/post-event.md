## IASc Annual Meeting Outreach, IISER Tirupati

Outreach activities were conducted at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Tirupati during the Indian Academy of Sciences 91st Annual Meeting, 14–15 November 2025.

The Annual Meeting featured a Public Lecture by Shivkumar Kalyanaraman, CEO, Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF), on "AI for science and engineering", alongside Presidential Address by Prof. Raghavan Varadarajan.
