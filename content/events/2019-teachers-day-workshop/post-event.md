On 12th September 2019, in collaboration with the Academy and ScienceUtsav, tAcT celebrated Teachers' Day. About 60 science teachers from various schools in and around Bengaluru participated in a full-day science workshop featuring hands-on experiments and interactive lectures on pedagogy.

Karima Anjum from Royal Society of Chemistry showed the teachers how to conduct experiments using simple chemical solutions on commonly found materials. Dr. B. R. Gopal, Ex-Principal of MES Teachers Training College, shared valuable nuggets of his 30-plus years of experience with the teachers. Prof. Ganesha Bhatta, former Principal of MES Teachers College CTE, challenged the participants to come up with simple science experiments for students using everyday objects like marbles, throw-away plastic cups, etc.
