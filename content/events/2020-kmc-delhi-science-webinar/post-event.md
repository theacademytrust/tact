A 3-day science webinar was jointly organised by Kirori Mal College, Delhi, The Academy Trust (tAcT), and the Indian Academy of Sciences, from 9 to 11 November 2020.

**Programme:**
- **9 November 2020**, Saumitra Das (Director, National Institute of Biomedical Genomics): "Hepatitis C Virus: Life in Liver"
- **10 November 2020**, V. Nagaraja (Professor, Indian Institute of Science): "Gene-Editing Technologies: Fallout of an Arms Race in the Bacterial World"
- **11 November 2020**, Partha P. Majumder (National Science Chair, Govt. of India; President, Indian Academy of Sciences): "Data Science in Understanding Human Health and Disease"

Time: 4 PM – 5:30 PM each day
