## Raman Science Festival — National Science Day 2021

On 28 February 2021, The Academy Trust (tAcT) and Science Utsav jointly celebrated National Science Day with the Raman Science Festival, webcast live from IASc Bengaluru.

**Highlights:**
- Welcome and significance of the day: Prof. Partha P. Majumder, tAcT President
- Math game for the audience: Prof. Tirthankar Bhattacharyya, Outreach Committee Chair
- Talk on Light: Prof. Rajaram Nityananda (Azim Premji University)
- Chemistry demonstrations for younger students: Shashank Karnam, ScienceUtsav
- STEM workshop: Ramakrishnan Iyer, MakerScientist
- AI and Engineering demonstrations: Parag, Cretile
- Experiments: Jyoti Parruck, Delhi chapter, Royal Society of Chemistry
- Science games and Quiz contest: Akshay
- Coding workshop: Harsha Atri
- Science-themed magic: MakerMasti

**Reach:**
- Registrations: 3,200+
- Average participants per session: 600+
- Live online audience: 4,600+
- Total online views: 13,000+
