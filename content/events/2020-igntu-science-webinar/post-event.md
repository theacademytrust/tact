## IGNTU–tAcT Science Webinar

A science webinar was jointly organised by Indira Gandhi National Tribal University (IGNTU), Amarkantak, Madhya Pradesh, and The Academy Trust (tAcT), Indian Academy of Sciences, Bengaluru, on 28 September 2020.

**Speakers:**
- **Prof. N. Sathyamurthy** — "The Chemical Bond" (3:30 PM – 4:30 PM)
- **Prof. S. Ananthakrishnan**, Electronic Science Dept, S.P. Pune University — "Sky beyond the human eyes: Looking at the Universe through radio window" (4:50 PM – 5:50 PM)

The webinar was conducted via Google Meet, YouTube, and Facebook Live.
