Students of Chiguru Srujana Shaale, Hosabidarahalli, H.D. Kote taluk, visited Bengaluru for a three-day Vijnana Aranya from 30 October to 1 November 2025.

**30 October 2025**
- 5:30 PM: Arrival at Academy's Jalahalli Guest House
- 6:30–7:30 PM: Harate by V.S.S. Sastry

**31 October 2025**
- 9:30 AM–1 PM: Curated tour of VITM (Rama Srinidhi and Prasanta Biswal)
- 2:30–5 PM: Tour of Raman Museum; Harate by V.S.S. Sastry, Achutha and Anandaram

**1 November 2025**
- Breakfast at Guest House, followed by departure to Hosabidarahalli
