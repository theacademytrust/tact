## Vijnana Aranya, Mysuru — Talks by Nemichandra

A two-day Vijnana Aranya was conducted across multiple venues in Mysuru on 7–8 November 2025. The main presenter was Ms. Nemichandra, Writer, Biographer, Speaker, and Former General Manager, HAL.

**7 November 2025, Friday**

- Shakthidhama Vidyashala (10:30 AM – 12 PM): "Tale of Two Scientists: Marie Curie and Sir C.V. Raman" — for high school students
- Jnanodaya PU College (2:30–4 PM): "Emotional Resilience and Digital Wellness" — for PU students
- Indian Institute of Educational Theatre (6–7:30 PM): "Rediscovering Women Scientists" — for general public

**8 November 2025, Saturday**

- Divya Jyothi Charitable Trust (10:30 AM – 12 PM): "Lead, Kindly Light — Inspirational Real-life Stories" — for visually challenged PU girl students
- 12 PM – 2 PM: Mathematical concepts through Origami — V.S.S. Sastry, Math & Origami instructor
