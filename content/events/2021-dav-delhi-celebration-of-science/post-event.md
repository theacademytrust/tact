## A Celebration of Science, DAV Public School Delhi

A two-day online Celebration of Science was jointly organised by DAV Public School, The Academy Trust (tAcT), and the Indian Academy of Sciences on 27–28 August 2021.

**27 August 2021**
- Purpose of the Celebration — Partha P. Majumder, President, Indian Academy of Sciences (4:50 PM)
- The Prime Numbers — Tirthankar Bhattacharyya, Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru (5:00 PM)
- Biology in the 21st century and how we got here — Amitabh Joshi, JNCASR, Bengaluru (6:00 PM)

**28 August 2021**
- Some physics discoveries in the last decade: Gravitational Waves, Higgs Boson, and Photographing Black Holes — Bedangadas Mohanty, NISER Bhubaneswar (5:00 PM)
- Chemistry is Fun! — Uday Maitra, Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru (6:00 PM)
