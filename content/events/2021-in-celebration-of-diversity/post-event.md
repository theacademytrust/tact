"In Celebration of Diversity" was a six-week online lecture series jointly organised by The Academy Trust (tAcT) and the National Council of Science Museums (NCSM), from 1 October to 10 November 2021. All sessions were held on Fridays at 4:00 PM.

**Programme:**

- **1 October 2021**, Welcome: Prof. Tirthankar Bhattacharyya (tAcT Outreach Committee Chair); Why celebrate diversity?: Prof. Partha P. Majumder (President, Indian Academy of Sciences)
- **8 October 2021**, Physical Diversity in India: Prof. Somnath Dasgupta (IISc Bengaluru); Science Promotion by NCSM: Arijit Dutta Choudhury (Director-General, NCSM)
- **15 October 2021**, Animal Diversity in India: Prof. R. Sukumar (IISc Bengaluru)
- **22 October 2021**, Plant Diversity in India: Prof. Subhra Chakraborty (NIPGR, Delhi)
- **29 October 2021**, Ecological Diversity in India: Prof. Renee V. Borges and Prof. Madhusree Bandyopadhyay
- **10 November 2021**, Linguistic Diversity in India: Prof. G.N. Devy (Founder, People's Linguistic Survey of India); Human History & Diversity in India: Prof. Analabha Basu (ISI Kolkata); Concluding Remarks: Prof. Renee V. Borges; Vote of Thanks: Manash Bagchi (Curator, NCSM)
