## Science Talk at Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Bengaluru

An online science talk was organised by tAcT for students and teachers of Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Bengaluru, on 29 June 2020.

**Speaker:** Prof. Partha Pratim Majumder

The session drew around 68 participants, including invited guests. Important questions related to the virus and its possible implications were addressed during the Q&A.
