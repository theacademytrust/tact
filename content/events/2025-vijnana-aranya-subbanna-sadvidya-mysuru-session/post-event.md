## Vijnana Aranya, Mysuru — Preeminent Women Mathematicians

A Vijnana Aranya was conducted across two venues in Mysuru on 20 December 2025, in connection with National Mathematics Day.

**T.S. Subbanna Smaraka Girls' High School, Mysuru (10:30 AM – 12 PM)**
- "Preeminent Women Mathematicians: From Hypatia to Maryam Mirzakhani" — Ms. Nemichandra, Writer, Biographer, Former GM, HAL (for 9th and 10th grade students)
- "Kanakanadalli Ganitha" — Harsha, Maths museum curator and instructor (for 8th grade students)

**Sadvidya Semi-Residential PU College, Mysuru (2–3:30 PM)**
- "Preeminent Women Mathematicians: From Hypatia to Maryam Mirzakhani" — Ms. Nemichandra (for PU students)
