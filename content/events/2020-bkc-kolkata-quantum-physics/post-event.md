A national webinar on Quantum and Particle Physics was jointly organised by The Academy Trust (tAcT) and Brahmananda Keshab Chandra (BKC) College, Kolkata, on 29 August 2020.

**Speakers:**
- **Prof. Srubabati Goswami** (Physical Research Laboratory), "Neutrinos: The invisible messengers from Space"
- **Prof. Sushanta Dattagupta**, "Can Graphene be used to teach Quantum mechanics?"

Session chaired by Prof. KB Sinha, Outreach Committee Chair.

The webinar was conducted via Zoom and YouTube Live, open to all.
