## Vijnana Harate, Anantha Vidyaniketan — IASc and RRI Visit

60 students of 9th grade from Anantha Vidyaniketan visited the Indian Academy of Sciences and RRI, Bengaluru, on 22 July 2025.

**Programme:**
- 9:00–9:30 AM: Introduction and refreshments
- 9:30–11:00 AM: Vijnana Harate by Prof. Seetha
- 11:00 AM–1:00 PM: Hands-on activities by Agastya International Foundation team
- 1:00–2:00 PM: Lunch
- 2:00–3:30 PM: Curated visit to Raman Museum and RRI Archives Gallery
