A science outreach programme titled 'Karnataka's Wildlife' was held at Vanita Sadana, Krishnamurthypuram, Mysore, from 11 AM to 1 PM on 4 August 2018. The programme was organized for high school students.

Dr. Mewa Singh, a wildlife scientist, spoke on elephants and monkeys, and Manu, a naturalist from Mysore, spoke on birds.
