## Ganitha Mela at VITM, Bengaluru

A Ganitha Mela (Mathematics Fair) was held at the Visvesvaraya Industrial and Technological Museum (VITM), Bengaluru, on 22 December 2025, as part of National Mathematics Day (Ramanujan Day) celebrations.

Jointly organised by The Academy Trust (tAcT), Seed2Sapling Education, VITM-NCSM, and IISER Tirupati, the Ganitha Mela featured activity stalls with mathematical toys, puzzles and games, demonstrations, Harates with scientists and educators, and cultural programmes.
