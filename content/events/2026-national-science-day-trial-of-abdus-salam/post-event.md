## National Science Day — The Trial of Abdus Salam

On 28 February 2026 (National Science Day), tAcT and Seed2Sapling Education organised an outing for teachers and educators to see the science play "The Trial of Abdus Salam" at Rangashankara, Bengaluru.

16 teachers and educators from various parts of Karnataka attended, with sponsorship from Ridhdhi Foundation. The event aimed to raise scientific awareness among educators through a creative, dramatic medium.
