A webinar on Evolutionary and Organismal Biology was jointly organised by the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Rajasthan, and The Academy Trust (tAcT), Indian Academy of Sciences, Bengaluru, on 7 June 2020.

**Speaker:** Prof. Amitabh Joshi, Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research (JNCASR), Bengaluru

**Moderator and Opening Remarks:** Ms. Mugdha Sinha, IAS, Secretary, Science & Technology, Govt. of Rajasthan

The session was conducted online for college and school students.
