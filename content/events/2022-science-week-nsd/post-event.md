## Science Week, National Science Day 2022

The Academy Trust (tAcT), Indian Academy of Sciences, in collaboration with Seed2Sapling Education, organised "Science Week" from 26 February to 6 March 2022, to celebrate National Science Day.

The week-long programme consisted of eight 90-minute online sessions open to all, with specific sessions for students and for schoolteachers.

**Expert talks:**
- Inaugural Address: Prof. Umesh V Waghmere, President, IASc Council (26 Feb)
- Change and Necessity: Emergence of Excellence in Science, Prof. Dipankar Chatterji, IISc (26 Feb)
- How primes keep secrets, Dr. Kaneenika Sinha, IISER Pune (28 Feb)
- Science, Technology and Innovation for Sustainable Clean Water, Prof. Pradeep Thallapil, IIT Madras (28 Feb)
- Overview of Particle Physics: From the heart of the matter to the edge of the Universe, Prof. Rohini Godbole, IISc (5 Mar)

**Exploratory sessions (Seed2Sapling Education):**
- Science of Everyday Things: Explorations with Ice, Dr. Vyshnavi Ramesh (27 Feb)
- How to bring out the Joy of Science in Class?, Dr. Mrinal Shah and Dr. Pankaj Jain (26 Feb)
- How to bring out the Joy of Maths in Class?, Dr. K M Shivkumar (4 Mar)
- Magic of Math through Paper Folding, Dr. Ashwin Guha (6 Mar)

**Quest Fest:** A question and observation contest for students and teachers, running until 20 March 2022.
