The Academy Trust (tAcT)

The Academy Trust is a public charitable trust set up in 2014 by the Indian Academy of Sciences, which was founded in 1934 by Nobel laureate Sir C. V. Raman. It was created to make science education more tangible in schools, colleges, and in society at large.

tAcT works through three strands: science education outreach programmes such as Vijnana Harate, Vijnana Aranya, Vijnana Yuvati, Vijnana Nataka and Ganitha Mela; internships that link students, academia, and industry; and an endowment Chair Professorship that brings leading scientists from across the world to India to share their knowledge and expertise.

Programs that make a TANGIBLE change

Vijnana Harate

Informal interactions between scientists/educators and students and general public in educational institutions and community spaces.

Vijnana Aranya

Outreach visits in rural, tribal, and remote areas, often in partnership with local schools and community organisations.

Vijnana Yuvati

Skill-oriented outreach to benefit young women.

Vijnana Nataka

Outreach through the medium of theatre and music.

Ganitha Mela

Exploring Mathematics through a variety of activities including games and cultural and entertainment programs.
