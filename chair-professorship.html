The Springer-Nature Chair

Background

The Springer-Nature Chair is tied to the Indian Academy of Sciences' publishing agreement with Springer-Nature. It is an endowment-linked programme that uses that relationship to support scientific exchange within India.

How it works

An eminent scientist is invited to visit India as the Springer-Nature Chair. During the visit, they deliver lectures and hold scientific interactions at research institutions and universities across India in their area of expertise.

A typical Chair visit

Chair appointment

An eminent scientist is selected and invited to India as the Springer-Nature Chair.

Lectures

The Chair delivers public and institutional lectures connected to their research area.

Scientific interactions

The visit includes direct meetings and discussions with scientists at research institutions across India.
